# 🧠 Product Analytics Module

## 📌 Overview

This repository showcases my work and learning journey through the **Product Analytics Module**, where I gained hands-on experience and a solid understanding of what it means to work as a **Product Analyst**. I explored how analysts collaborate with cross-functional teams—product managers, designers, researchers, and engineers—to provide insights that drive product improvements.

## 👩‍💻 My Experience

I learned that product analysts play a crucial role across different company types:

- In **startups**, they often take on a wide range of tasks—from product to marketing and even financial analysis.
- In **larger companies** like *Uber, Google, Wix,* or *Vinted*, they tend to focus on specific product areas, offering deep insights based on data.

Regardless of the company size, the primary mission is always the same: **use data to improve products and enhance user experience**.

## 🔍 Key Analysis Techniques I Practiced

### ✅ Funnel Analysis  
I learned how to visualize user flows and identify where users drop off.  
*Example: I calculated the conversion rate from product view to favorite to evaluate feature adoption.*

### ✅ Ad-Hoc Analysis  
I worked on answering urgent business questions, like investigating unexpected KPI changes or recreating user journeys to support UX research.

### ✅ A/B Testing  
I practiced designing and analyzing experiments—from defining success metrics to interpreting results and drawing conclusions.

### ✅ KPI & OKR Definition  
I gained experience in identifying success metrics and setting up tracking systems to monitor product performance over time.

### ✅ User Segmentation  
I started with basic active user logic and progressed to more advanced segmentation based on user behavior and intent.

## 🧾 Working with Event Data

I primarily worked with **event-level data**, which included:

- **Frontend interactions** (e.g., button clicks)
- **Backend events** (e.g., order placements)

I got familiar with querying pre-processed datasets using SQL, as well as modeling raw event data (like parsing JSON) when needed.

## 🎯 What I Accomplished

- Gained a deep understanding of product analyst responsibilities  
- Practiced core analysis types used in the field  
- Worked with real event data and performed exploratory analysis  
- Applied my skills to solve life-like problems  
- Improved my ability to think critically about product performance and strategy
